# Description

The lower runoff channels beneath the station have slowed to a crawl. Wastewater is backing up into habitable zones, and the filtration rigs are beginning to choke. A collapsed section of tunnel is suspected, but something else is moving down there, something large enough to keep displacing debris after every attempted clearing.

Clear the blockage and ensure water flow is fully restored. If a creature is present, remove it or drive it off.

If the sewer fails, disease follows. [[Factions + Groups/Hood Station/Hood Station|Hood Station]] cannot afford an outbreak, and rerouting waste would cripple water reserves needed for drinking and hydroponics.

### Completion Requirements

- [x] find and clear the blockage
- [x] drive off any creatures, if applicable
