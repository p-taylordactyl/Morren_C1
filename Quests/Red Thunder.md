# Description

Both [[Thorbin Sprake|Thorbin]] and [[Az]] have heard of Red Thunder. 

Az would like you to secure a trade deal with its creator to supply the [[The Troupe|Troupe]] -- he says he knows an Archotech Gadgetmaker who would easily be enticed to join [[Hood Station]] if we have a healthy supply of Red Thunder.

Thorbin knows of an Enchanter looking for work but will not come anywhere near Hood Station if the nearby Red Thunder supply isn't eliminated due to fear of relapse should it be available.


### Completion Requirements

Visit [[Morgo]], the maker of Red Thunder, in the [[BB Megatower]] and either:

- [ ] make a trade deal to supply the Troupe with Red Thunder

OR

- [ ]  halt his production of Red Thunder and eliminate his existing supply
