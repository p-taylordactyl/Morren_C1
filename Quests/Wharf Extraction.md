# Description

There is key data within the [[Harbormaster's Office]], on the [[The Rust Mammoth|three]] [[The Gray Pelican|docked]] [[Dockrunner|vessels]] and within the [[Clerk's Home]] that when combined can be leveraged by one of the interested parties to one of two ends.

[[Cillian Kravus|Cillian]] wishes to reroute rare and valuable materials used in Spell Focus Modifications so that the spellcasters of [[Hood Station]] can specialize to improve their field performance. 

[[Vinnie Brassjaw|Vinnie]] wishes to use the data to expand his reach and utilize this new network to run down leads for both himself and his allies.
