# Description

Rumors of the [[NPCs/The Driftwarden|Driftwarden]] have caught the ear of both [[Ren Calder|Ren]] and [[Rosetta Thorne|Miss Rosie]] -- each hoping to host the individual at [[Hood Station]] to different ends. 

Rosie has hear his blood contains magical properties and if we had his hear, it can be used to indefinitely cheapen the cost of performing miracles at the [[Iron Form Dojo|Chojo]].

Ren has heard that he was once a weapon smith of great renown, and would like to learn his secrets so that Hood Station can begin to modify their own gear.

### Completion Requirements

- [ ]  Bring the Driftwarden (or his body?) to Miss Rosie

OR

- [ ] Bring the Driftwarden (or his knowledge of equipment mods) to Ren Calder
