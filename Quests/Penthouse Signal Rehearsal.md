# Description

A broadcast has begun spilling across station frequencies from the long-sealed penthouse at the tower's peak. It carries layered music, fragmented monologues, distorted applause, and repeating stage directions, all spliced together like a performance stuck in rehearsal. [[The Troupe]] claims the signal matches a lost production they once staged, one that was abandoned when the penthouse was shut down. 

They insist no one currently has access to that space, and none of their members have been inside in years. Someone, or something, is using their work without permission, reshaping it into something unstable. 

Reach the penthouse and locate the source of the broadcast. Determine whether it is a hijacked archive, a rogue performer, or an unknown presence imitating the Troupe's work. Secure or shut down the signal as directed by The Troupe.

### Completion Requirements

- [x] Identify the source of the broadcast
- [x] Secure and/or shut down the signal - contact Troupe for direction if needed
