# The Zodiac Calendar

The constellations that fill the night sky of Minastria are widely believed to exert some influence over mortal fate. From these beliefs arose the Zodiac Calendar, a divinatory system said to offer insight into an individual’s life path. While many diviners uphold its accuracy, critics point to countless exceptions and contradictions. Those who fail to exhibit the expected traits of their sign are often described as misaligned, existing out of harmony with the greater cosmic pattern.

The zodiac itself is a belt-shaped region of the heavens extending roughly eight degrees north and south of the ecliptic, the apparent yearly path of the sun across the celestial sphere.

Within this celestial band also travel the moon and the brightest planets, each following its own orbital rhythm. The zodiac is divided into twelve equal signs, each spanning thirty degrees of celestial longitude. These signs correspond loosely to the major constellations: Arcanus, Floralis, Solarius, Aqualis, Ventorus, Terranus, Ignitus, Nebulus, Mystara, Lunaris, Umbra, and Stellaris.

Together, they form a symbolic cycle of time, personality, and cosmic alignment.

## Birth Chart Interpretations

Diviners claim that one’s zodiac sign shapes personality, temperament, and spiritual alignment. These interpretations are common across most schools of astrology, though their accuracy remains contested.

Arcanus: Those born under Arcanus are often regarded as natural spellcasters, possessing an innate sensitivity to arcane forces and a tendency toward introspection and study.

Floralis: Floralis individuals are patient and creative, often drawn to cultivation, healing, and the slow shaping of growth in both people and places.

Solarius: Solarius-born are seen as natural leaders, marked by confidence, vitality, and a strong presence in moments of crisis.

Aqualis: Aqualis individuals are empathetic and emotionally perceptive, adapting easily to change and forming deep personal bonds.

Ventorus: Those of Ventorus are restless thinkers and explorers, driven by curiosity and a desire for new ideas, horizons, and experiences.

Terranus: Terranus people are steady and reliable, often serving as stabilizing figures within families, communities, and institutions.

Ignitus: Ignitus-born are defined by intensity and ambition, propelled by powerful inner drives and a refusal to yield easily.

Nebulus: Nebulus individuals are imaginative and fluid in thought, embracing uncertainty and innovation with ease.

Mystara: Mystara people are spiritually inclined, drawn toward hidden truths, visions, and the unseen dimensions of reality.

Lunaris: Lunaris-born are reflective and emotionally rich, finding meaning in care, memory, and quiet understanding.

Umbra: Umbra individuals are perceptive strategists, excelling in subtlety, planning, and navigating complex situations.

Stellaris: Those born under Stellaris are idealists and visionaries, often inspiring others through hope, creativity, and symbolic thinking.
