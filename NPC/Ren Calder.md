# Description

An active member of [[The Redacted]] who is constantly searching for leads and opportunities to push back against the oligarchs. He’s young and a bit naive, but genuinely committed to the cause. Ren sends a high volume of tips to [[Maximilius Adkins|Max]], many of which go unused, but he was the one who connected Max to [[Keena O'Malley|Keena]]’s story and, by extension, [[Factions + Groups/Hood Station/Hood Station|Hood Station]]. Since then, Max has taken Ren’s contributions more seriously, though he still tries to temper some of his more reckless ideas.
