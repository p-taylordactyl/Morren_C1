# Backstory

Keena is a human woman in her late teens/early twenties. She's originally from [[Westreach]] and for most of her youth, lived in a small apartment with her parents, brother, aunt, uncle, and two cousins. However, her mother was sickly and couldn't hold a steady job, and usually had to stay home with other family members. [[The West Valence Saints]] eventually forced them out of their home and drafted her father, brother, and uncle into gang labor. Afraid of the conditions of gang labor, the violence and control of the Saints, and their lack of housing, the adults made an escape plan and moved the family into the nearby [[Cloud District]].

Conditions were tough, with the family back to living together in a small apartment and working grueling hours at the nearby [[Titan Corporation]] Factory Complex. However, in an accident that would end up injuring or killing over 50 people at the factory, her cousin [[Róisín Ward]] would lose her arm and her brother [[Lorcan O'Malley]] would lose his life. The Titan Corporation downplayed the accident as a "small-scale mishap" and blamed the workers for negligence, refusing to pay any compensation for those injured or killed in the accident and firing anyone who didn't show up for their next shift. The O'Malley-Ward family ended up losing their home again, as they were down two more wage-earners, and moved into Hood Station where her uncle, [[Cillian Ward]], would open a dojo.

Angry and lost without her dear older brother, she was visiting his grave/talking to him at the small memorial shrine for him at their dojo when he appeared before her. He revealed himself as an aspect of [[Morrenel]], and told her what had happened at the factory was a perversion of justice and those responsible must pay. He told her if she promised to hunt down the unrighteous in his name, to repair the broken chain of justice, he would gift her with power. Desperate to avenge her brother and right the wrongs, she agreed.

In her free time, she studies martial arts and various fighting styles with her uncle, even teaching some classes to women and younger kids, and hunts down those who haven't been punished for injustice, corruption, or exploitation, using their wealth or power to escape the ramifications of their actions. Sometimes, after hours at the dojo, she even meets with others who also believe in the [[Covenant of Equal Measure]] where they have scripture study, religious debates, and so forth, though they keep the group fairly small and not many are aware of it. While some might think her delirious after the loss of her brother, becoming one of Morrenel's Chosen settled something within Keena and gave her renewed purpose in the world.

**Themes**: Purpose; Justice; Truth; Hope Against Rot; Faith vs Fury; From Below, Judgment Rises; The Necessary Blade; Ambition; Survival; Equality; The Common People; Family; Optimism vs Realism; Legality vs Morality

# PC Bonds

**[[Maximilius Adkins|Max Adkins]]** (Major): Max and Keena met as a consequence of the accident at the Titan Corporation Factory Complex. Keena was posting about it online, and his network connected the two so he could interview her. He started an investigation into the accident, eventually identifying the culprit ([[Augustin Devereaux]]) who caused and covered up the accident. Together, they went to interview him, with two purposes in mind: learn more about the inner workings and corruption of the Titan Corporation, and exact revenge for causing the accident that took her brother's life. After beating him into a coma, the pair realized they could work together in order to expose the truth about the corrupt oligarchs exploiting the working class and to bring justice to those corrupt and powerful people.

**[[Katriel Buchanon]]** (Minor): Kat hangs out at the [[Iron Form Dojo|Dojo]] fairly often, coming to spar or take classes. Keena and Kat are aware of each other and friendly, but not much more than acquaintances who see each other ~lifting at the gym~.

# NPC Bonds

**[[Ren Calder]]**. A member of Max's informant network and an occasional student at Keena's self defense classes. Although they were not close enough to feel comfortable talking to Keena about the accident or skilled enough to do a formal interview, they knew her story was real and contacted Max to let him know her stories were real and not some tinfoil hat conspiracy theory, that he should come to the Cloud District to meet and talk with Keena. As Max and Keena became friends, Ren felt more comfortable with her and came to more of her classes, though they never revealed their part in getting Max to Hood Station. Keena considers Ren something like her mentee, as far as marital arts goes.

**[[Cillian Kravus]]**, the camera guy who works with Max.

**[[Kairo Voss]]** (they/them) and **[[Nyra Lightwell]]** (she/her). Two of her friends and members of (both parts of) the Chojo.

# Messages from "Morronel"

## Candle Vision

Keena sees an image of a candle, along with the following message:

"Keena, you will not know what these words mean, and you won't if you fail the trials ahead. But on the road ahead, you will find candles. You must take them, and take them to the houses of the wicked. Light them at their doorsteps, and what was lost will be found."

## Temple Instruction

No imagery, but Keena hears the words:

"A sinner walks among you. Do with them what you will. Reap the harvest which you have sown."
