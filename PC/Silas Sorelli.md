# Backstory

Silas is a tiefling man in his mid-30's, originally from the [[Flooded Zone]]. Since his childhood, Silas has always possessed a brilliant mind, a knack for deceit, and ambitions beyond his station. Growing up, Silas was fascinated by the arts of illusion, hypnotism, sleight-of-hand, and the power they held over reality and the mortal perspective of it-- driving him to learn the arts by any means he had available. His all-consuming fixation would lead him to infiltrate arcane lecture halls, steal magical tomes, and knowingly lose to hustled games of "chance" by street magicians just to study their ways.

Silas was not yet an adult before he conjured his first illusion, which only served to fuel his relentless pursuits. He and his childhood friend [[Kali Torreo|Kali]] would grift their way into affording stolen black market spell books he could barely read and they would practice his illusions and enchantments on anyone unfortunate enough to be caught in Silas' whims, the two of them outwitting and escaping the police at every failure due to his layers upon layers of meticulously crafted escape plans. By the time he became an adult, Silas had mastered the art of Silent Image to a degree very few master Illusionists could comprehend. He was a bonified prodigy-- he knew it-- and nothing felt out of reach.

That is until the [[Ghosts of the Flooded Zone]] took control of the district, and his childhood friend Kali was swallowed by their beliefs. Throughout their time growing up, Silas had always been the head of the dynamic duo, and Kali had faithfully followed his lead. Together, they had accomplished great things-- at least according to Silas. Always an independent thinker, he had not considered the environment Kali was in while not together, and in what felt like the briefest of moments when Silas wasn't looking, Kali had fallen victim to their propaganda. Either through her parents, siblings, or maybe other friends if she had any, Kali had slowly but surely been consumed by the ideals of the terrorist cell and when Silas made what felt like the obvious move to leave the Flooded Zone, Kali had already joined the cause. She had been inundated by hatred for the oligarchs and isolationist xenophobia, refusing to leave with him. 

Unable to convince her or even convince himself that the Ghosts were entirely in the wrong, Silas instead committed himself and his talents to shattering the oligarchy and their hold over society-- and the Ghosts of the Flooded Zone along with it-- by any means he could so that one day, Kali could be freed from her mental prison and join him on the other side of reality.


# PC Bonds

**[[Dorian Caldwell]]** (Major) - Introduced to Silas through their mutual acquaintance, Rosetta Thorne. Dorian was put forth as the "perfect example" of what we could mold the children into being. His talents in regards to subterfuge, infiltration, and information gathering were something the children could aspire to become. During their time working together, Dorian and Silas came to see each other as close friends first and business associates second. Unbeknownst to them, the children's respect and admiration towards Silas would be used as a manipulation tactic by [[Rosetta Thorne|Rosetta]] when they would see how close Dorian and Silas had become. If the children could be more like Dorian, maybe Silas would be their friend too...

**[[Gideon Oublier]]** (Minor) - In a world of grey, Gideon is a reminder that good people-- actually good people-- still exist. While Silas has resolved himself to fixing the city by the most "efficient" means, people like Gideon give him pause and help him stay on the right path. When one's ambitions (and ego) are as lofty as Silas's in a city like [[Old Valence]], it's easy to fall into doing things the easy way. The wrong way. Despite their differences and conflicting ideologies, Silas knows that Gideon and the good people in the world like him are the very thing he's fighting for, and it's nice to have a reminder nearby.


# NPC Bonds

**[[Rosetta Thorne]]** - Originally known to Silas as "Miss Rosie" from the Hood Station community, she eventually approached and revealed to him her true self: Rosetta "The Thorn" Thorne, an elderly human woman who in her prime was a devoted member of [[The Bellanova Family]] on her meteoric rise to notoriety and power before she was framed and abandoned. She put forth to him the idea of building the [[Factions + Groups/Hood Station/Hood Station|Hood Station]] orphanage into the spy network that eventually became the [[The Protective Association for Wayward and Neglected Strays|Association]], with the goal of eventually having a strong enough information network to bring down the Bellanova Family and the other oligarchs. Her attempts at manipulation were obvious to him-- such as naming him the "King" of the Association and praising his brilliant mind and ambitions-- but he felt that if he didn't take the bait, someone else would. Silas ignored her obviously nefarious intentions and agreed to Rosetta's plans, arrogantly believing that he alone had the capacity to guide the children, never stray the path, and achieve something great. She would eventually introduce Silas to Dorian, who would go onto become his closest friend and the only person Silas feels he can truly trust with his secrets.

**[[Kali Torreo]]** - A childhood friend of Silas who stayed behind in the Flooded Zone when he left. Unlike Silas, who saw the Ghosts of the Flooded Zone as being too short-sighted and extremist-- not addressing the real problem of the Oligarchy-- Kali was fully indoctrinated into the Ghosts' ideology. Despite growing up together and caring for her immensely, Silas was unable to convince her to leave the Flooded Zone with him. Rather than stay for her sake at the cost of his own ideals, Silas left her behind with the hopes of one day breaking the chains the Ghosts had placed on her.
