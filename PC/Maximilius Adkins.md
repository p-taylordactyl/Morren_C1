# Backstory

Maximilius Adkins (Max) started his career at [[Crownlight Media]] as a junior field documentarian. He was competent at the job, good at capturing events as they unfolded and identifying the parts of a story that would actually resonate with an audience. During the [[Titan Corporation|Titan]] Line Blackout, he was assigned to cover the [[Flooded Zone]] response efforts. He recorded evacuations, stranded residents, and people refusing relocation in the aftermath. When the material aired, the final broadcast presented a controlled narrative: orderly response, successful recovery, minimal disruption. Large portions of his footage were removed or reframed beyond recognition. It was not the first time Max had seen editorial shaping, but it was the first time he fully realized how completely recorded reality could be replaced.

Not long after, he left Crownlight and began working independently across [[Old Valence]], salvaging what equipment he could and documenting conditions outside institutional coverage. He moved through districts taking on small assignments, trading footage for shelter, and gradually building a presence on underground forums and arcane message boards under various handles. Over time, people began sending him leads, fragments of events, and local accounts that never reached official channels.

As that network grew, Max’s work shifted into a combination of structured informational pieces and raw field recordings, some edited and contextualized, others intentionally unfiltered. The goal wasn’t to control interpretation, but to make real conditions harder to ignore or fully erase. These materials circulated through informal channels and slowly built a loose, decentralized information web connected to his efforts.

One of those connections, [[Ren Calder]], passed along a lead involving [[Keena O'Malley|Keena]], who had lost her brother in an unreported incident at a Titan Corporation facility. Keena herself was from [[Locations/Hood Station|Hood Station]], and through her, Max was first introduced to the settlement and the conditions people were living under there.

The lack of accountability and official denial around the [[Titan Corporation]] case led Max and Keena to investigate together. As they worked through the details, they identified [[Augustin Devereaux]] as responsible for both the incident and its suppression. When they confronted him, the situation escalated and ended with Devereaux left in a coma. Afterward, Max and Keena continued working together on similar cases, focused on exposing unreported incidents and pushing against corporate impunity.

In the process, Hood Station became more than just another lead for Max. At the time, he was effectively living as a nomad across Old Valence, sleeping where he could, trading work or footage for temporary shelter, and relying on unstable networks of contacts. Hood Station offered something different: a place that operated outside of official visibility, where information gaps were constant but so was the need for them to be filled.

Seeing that overlap, Max began using his work to support and strengthen those information gaps in both directions, bringing reliable external reporting into Hood Station while also documenting its realities for the outside world in a controlled, contextualized way. Over time, this developed into The Redacted, a decentralized network built around combining structured reporting with raw documentation. The network now operates across Old Valence, with Hood Station serving as a key anchor point for grounding information and, occasionally, for quietly receiving those who arrive after encountering their work.

# NPC Bonds

**[[Ren Calder]]** – An active member of The Redacted who is constantly searching for leads and opportunities to push back against the oligarchs. He’s young and a bit naive, but genuinely committed to the cause. Ren sends a high volume of tips to Max, many of which go unused, but he was the one who connected Max to Keena’s story and, by extension, Hood Station. Since then, Max has taken Ren’s contributions more seriously, though he still tries to temper some of his more reckless ideas.

**[[Cillian Kravus]]** – One of the first people Max connected with through The Redacted, both online and in person. They quickly recognized a shared mindset: a drive to expose the truth while still creating compelling, high-quality pieces. Before long, they began working together regularly, forming a natural partnership, Max as the front-facing voice and operator, and Cillian handling the technical side, keeping recordings clean, systems running, and operations stable behind the scenes.

**[[Barron Sutton]]** – A former manager of a team adjacent to Max’s during his time at Crownlight Media. The two got along well professionally, and Barron has recently attempted to reestablish contact. Max is uncertain of his intentions—whether it’s an attempt to locate him on behalf of Crownlight or a genuine, sympathetic outreach. He wants to believe the latter, but for now, he considers it too much of a risk to respond.
