# Backstory

Katriel was raised by her widowed father, [[Hadrian Buchanon]] in a militant religious sect of the [[The West Valence Saints]] known as the [[The Dominion Crew]], who practiced their own form of the Doctrine of Ordained Authority. They controlled only a handful of buildings, which were ruled with an iron fist, and more violence than was common with other crews. 

In addition to the normal Saints rules, the Dominion had their own additional rules to enforce control over their own members as well as their block.  Chief among these rules was to respect the hierarchy, starting with her father up the chain to the Dominion Crew leader, [[Gorax]]. Even the oligarchs, since they could overpower anyone they chose.

As she grew up, she started to notice inconsistencies in Hadrian and the other Dominion members' practice of the many rules, but pointing it out only ever resulted in being told to square up against grown men twice her age, the resulting beatdown reinforcing her own weakness. More and more, she began to catch herself empathizing with the residents on her block, and began privately questioning if their treatment at the hands of the Saints was truly deserved. 
 
Kat fled Westreach at age 19, having failed her initiation ritual to become a ranking member of the Dominion by refusing to kill a man who wouldn't work for the gang. She narrowly made it into the Cloud District, injured and her motorcycle nearly totaled. Fortunately, [[Gideon Oublier|Gideon]] found her and, eager to be a hero, brought her to [[Locations/Hood Station|Hood Station]].

At the [[Morrgen's Community Center|Community Center]], the [[Morrgen Oublier|Oublier family]] welcomed Kat to Hood Station with open arms, helping her get acquainted with the station and adjust to life outside the strict hierarchy of the Dominion Saints. She got a job working at [[Endline Garage]] under the tutelage of [[Thorbin Sprake]] and actually likes her life now, fixing autos and helping people instead of beating and extorting them. However, despite having found this little pocket of good in the hellscape, Kat can't help but wonder when the other shoe is going to drop. 

# PC Bonds

**[[Gideon Oublier]]** - One of the first people she met in Hood Station, along with his family. His mother, Morrgen, really took Katriel under her wing. Now, with most of Kat's time spent at the community center when she isn't at the shop or the chojo, she thinks of Gideon as a brother.

**[[Keena O'Malley]]** - Keena and Kat cross paths fairly regularly at the dojo, where they both (along with their mutual friend Kairo) practice martial arts. They're friendly in a gym bro sort of way, but have never exchanged much more than small talk or form pointers.


# NPC Bonds

**[[Thorbin Sprake]]**: Owner of Endline Garage, Kat's boss/mentor. He helped Kat fix up her busted bike when she first came to the station and let her work at the shop to cover the parts cost. They ended up getting along so well that he asked her to stay on permanently to work on autos. The garage has become her happy place. 

**[[Morrgen Oublier]]**: Gideon's mother, who took an immediate shine to Katriel when she first found her way to Hood Station. The closest thing to a Mom that Kat has ever known. 

**[[Kairo Voss]]** (they/them): Kairo is a regular customer at Endline and first recommended that Kat come spar at Iron Form. They've become regular workout buddies and sometimes go out scrapping together. More recently, Kairo has hinted to Kat that she should stick around after-hours at the chojo, though they won't say more than that.
