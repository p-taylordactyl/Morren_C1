# Spellfire Initiate
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 36*  
![](CLI/backgrounds/img/spellfire-initiate.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores.** Constitution, Intelligence, Charisma  
- **Feat.** [Spellfire Spark](CLI/feats/spellfire-spark-frhof.md)  
- **Skill Proficiencies.** [Arcana](CLI/rules/skills.md#Arcana) and [Perception](CLI/rules/skills.md#Perception)  
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Choose one kind of [Gaming Set](CLI/items/gaming-set-xphb.md)  
- **Equipment.** Choose A or B: (A) [Gaming Set](CLI/items/gaming-set-xphb.md) (same as above), [Arcane Focus](CLI/items/arcane-focus-xphb.md) ([Crystal](CLI/items/crystal-xphb.md) or [Wand](CLI/items/wand-xphb.md)), 2 [Pouches](CLI/items/pouch-xphb.md), [Traveler's Clothes](CLI/items/travelers-clothes-xphb.md), 36 GP; or (B) 50 GP  

You bear the gift of spellfire: a rare form of magic that channels the raw power of the Weave. Wielding spellfire takes a heavy toll on the body. You've trained both mind and body to efficiently wield this sacred power.
