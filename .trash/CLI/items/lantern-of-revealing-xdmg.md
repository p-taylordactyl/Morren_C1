# Lantern of Revealing
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![](CLI/items/img/lantern-of-revealing.webp#right)


While lit, this hooded lantern burns for 6 hours on 1 pint of oil, shedding [Bright Light](CLI/rules/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb.md) in a 30-foot radius and [Dim Light](CLI/rules/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb.md) for an additional 30 feet. [Invisible](CLI/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) creatures and objects are visible as long as they are in the lantern's [Bright Light](CLI/rules/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb.md). You can take a [Utilize](CLI/rules/actions.md#Utilize) action to lower the hood, reducing the lantern's light to [Dim Light](CLI/rules/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb.md) in a 5-foot radius.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 275. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
