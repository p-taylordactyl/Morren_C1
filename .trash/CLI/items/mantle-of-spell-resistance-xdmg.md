# Mantle of Spell Resistance
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](CLI/items/img/mantle-of-spell-resistance.webp#right)


You have [Advantage](CLI/rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on saving throws against spells while you wear this cloak.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 276. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
