# Description

The Redacted began as the informal continuation of [[Maximilius Adkins]]’ work after leaving [[Crownlight Media]]. Once a junior field documentarian embedded within Crownlight’s reporting structure, Max became increasingly aware of the gap between recorded reality and published reality, particularly after a major citywide incident, the [[Titan Corporation|Titan]] Line Blackout, where his raw field footage was heavily altered before broadcast.

Disillusioned but not directionless, he left the corporation and began working independently across [[Old Valence]], documenting both overlooked conditions and actively suppressed events. He salvaged recording equipment and built a loose network of correspondents, informants, and forum-based contacts who shared firsthand observations from across the city.

Over time, these connections formed a decentralized communication web. Max began releasing a mix of structured informational pieces and raw, unfiltered field recordings, sometimes carefully compiled reports, other times direct, unedited fragments meant to preserve events exactly as they occurred. His work spread through underground forums, message boards, and arcane relay networks, gradually gaining traction among those frustrated with official accounts and institutional framing.

Rather than trying to control interpretation, he focused on making reality harder to fully erase or sanitize. The network that formed around this flow of material became known as The Redacted, a name reflecting both the censored nature of what they exposed and the fragmented, incomplete truths they worked to restore and circulate.

The Redacted exists to restore proximity between lived reality and public understanding in a city where institutional narratives routinely distort or sanitize conditions on the ground.

Rather than opposing censorship with counter-propaganda, they operate through direct documentation, producing both structured informational reports and raw, unfiltered field recordings. Together, these pieces build an accessible but unvarnished picture of what is actually happening across Old Valence.

Their work creates unintended pathways: people who recognize themselves in what they see, people displaced by the same systems being documented, and people searching for somewhere that reflects the truth they already suspect.

[[Hood Station/Hood Station|Hood Station]] becomes one of those pathways.

### Leadership

The Redacted has no formal leadership structure. It functions as a distributed network held together by shared intent rather than hierarchy, with individuals contributing information, footage, and analysis as they are able.

Max sits at the informal center of this system, not as a commander, but as a hub of coordination and momentum. His role is primarily catalytic: gathering leads, initiating projects, and pushing material into circulation. Others connect through him because he is consistently active, visible, and willing to do the work of organizing scattered threads into something coherent.

Influence within The Redacted is fluid and situational. Authority is not assigned, but earned in moments of contribution, clarity, or reliability. Max simply happens to be the most persistent point of convergence within that flow.
