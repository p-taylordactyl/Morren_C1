![[religion_archenemyoforder.webp]]The Archenemy of Order, often referred to as just The Archenemy, is the name given to the cabal of gods who stand in opposition to Morrenel’s Divine Contract. They reject its authority and the limitations it imposes, seeking a form of existence unbound by its structure.

The Divine Contract exerts pressure on any god who attempts to defy it. That pressure grows until resistance alone becomes unsustainable, diminishing the god’s influence and forcing submission. The Archenemy endures because its members align themselves with powers that exist beyond the Contract’s reach.

These powers are known as the Chaos Gods, entities believed to have originated from a previous universe. They have proved resistant to being bound by the Divine Contract and remain outside its jurisdiction. Their existence introduces a source of authority that the Contract cannot fully constrain.

Gods who join the Archenemy enter into subjugation under one of the Chaos Gods. This act shields them from the full force of the Contract, but it reshapes them in the process. Their independence is reduced, their power is redirected, and their role becomes defined by service. These transformed beings are known as Chaos Princes.

Chaos Princes serve as enforcers, commanders, and extensions of their patron’s will. They operate across divine and mortal spheres, advancing the influence of their Chaos God through deliberate and often systemic disruption. Each carries a fragment of their patron’s nature, expressed through action more than doctrine.

Rarely, when a mortal proves themselves as worthy enough to begin they are transformed into Chaos Lords - rarer still are those Chaos Lords who accomplish legendary feats in the name of Chaos and are rewarded with Princehood.
