The Canonist Concord focuses on pure interpretation of the Divine Contract and Morrenel’s abstract principles. They emphasize study, precedent, and textual precision, rejecting political or moral bias.

Key beliefs and practices:

- Text as Law: The Divine Contract’s wording is absolute; no interpretation should favor power, wealth, or sentiment.
    
- Scholarship and Judgment: Canonists serve as judges, mediators, and archivists, documenting divine and mortal actions to ensure precedent is honored.
    
- Iconography: Perfectly balanced geometric symbols, layered seals, and orbiting glyphs representing law, obligation, and cosmic restraint.
    
- Clergy Roles: Advisors in disputes, catalogers of divine precedent, educators for those studying cosmic and mortal law.
    
- Tone: Neutral, methodical, and almost clinical; devotion is intellectual rather than emotional.
    

The Canonist Concord commands respect across factions because their interpretations can decisively settle theological or legal disputes, even among powerful mortals and semi-divine actors.
