The Covenant of Equal Measure emphasizes that no one is above the law, not even gods. Morrenel’s Contract proves that authority is always bound by law, and mortals too must be held accountable.

Key beliefs and practices:

- Divine Accountability: Gods obey the Contract; mortals should do the same. Wealth, power, and privilege confer no exemption.
    
- Social Justice: Preaches resistance to injustice, corruption, and exploitation, drawing parallels between divine law and equitable treatment of mortals.
    
- Iconography: Symbols of balance, unbroken chains, and interconnected glyphs; often carved into walls, worn as jewelry, or painted in neighborhoods.
    
- Clergy Roles: Community organizers, moral arbiters, and educators; often mediate disputes in poor districts or lead public ceremonies emphasizing fairness.
    
- Tone: Inspirational, moralistic, and assertive, fostering hope and resistance without encouraging chaos.
    

This sect resonates with those living under oppressive conditions. While they rarely hold institutional power, their teachings encourage the belief that all actions—no matter who performs them—are accountable under law.
