# Description

Welcome to Old Valence, one of many sub-cities built on the island of Iser within the mega-city of Morren. Morren covers nearly ninety percent of the landmass of the Dors Archipelago. Each island is linked by colossal bridges built by the ancient Archons, each stretching for miles. After the fall of the Archons and the Shattering, the cities of the Archipelago used this inherited infrastructure to expand at an unprecedented pace, eventually spreading across the bridges until the islands merged into an unbroken sprawl of urban terrain.

Countless decades of mass conflict ended in the 9th Century when a single power rose to dominate Morren, the Democratic Nation of Valence. Valence became the heart of the mega-city and its most celebrated jewel. Over time, however, corporate oligarchs seized control, manipulating free markets and democratic systems to corrupt those in power into granting corporations more and more. This ushered in an era of unfettered anarcho-capitalism, when wealth became the only real measure of authority.

What had once been a near-utopian Morren descended into unrest. In the 15th Century The [[Oligarchs]] withdrew to vast floating cities above the skyline, ruling from a distance. From there, they intervene only when disorder threatens the vital infrastructure, particularly the great [[Energy Siphon Tower|Siphoning Towers]] that drain energy from the lower cities and funnel it upward. All other matters of law and survival are left to the people below to resolve on their own.

In the years leading up to the modern age many would try, but ultimately fail to resist the Oligarchs of Valence and now there is no one left that could truly put up a fight.  Focus has been reshifted to making the best out of the current situation.  For some this is the new norm.  For others, it is merely a means to bide time until weakness presents itself.

Now in the 21st century - Old Valence remains a shadow of its former self, as rival factions struggle to shape its future according to their own competing visions.
