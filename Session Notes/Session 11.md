# 1/11/2012

We resume inside the [[Quiet Place]]. 

[[Gideon Oublier|Gideon]] and [[Silas Sorelli|Silas]] suddenly get bee-ed.

We try to discuss our next move, but the conversation keeps being disrupted by the bonking outside. [[Katriel Buchanon|Kat]] goes out and tries to help the bonking guy so he'll go away. She covers the camera on the ATM with a spare shirt, then tries to hack the ATM so the bonk guy will stop bonking.

Kat determines the camera is still functional - good thing she covered it, but she thinks she might have still been seen. Maybe it's time to change disguises.

She also notices this ATM is owned by the [[Cael Foundation]]. Whoops.

The guy bonking the is name [[Stonespear]]. He's impatient about how long the hacking takes but Kat talks him down and is able to successfully undo the security mechanisms. When she asks for a finders fee, Stonespear says they'll split 50-50 and gives her 25 platinum. 

He leaves, saying he will find her later so they can do more crime. No phone number needed, as he can apparently find her by smell.

The party talks about how that was probably a bit of a mistake.

As we're talking, we see a [[Orgo|guy]] breaking down near the busted empty ATM. Kat immediately feels bad and goes to him, planning to give him whatever platinum he needs, until he says he needs it to buy drugs. We ask what kind. He wants to buy a drug called Red Thunder which is apparently quite expensive - 10 platinum.

[[Maximilius Adkins|Max]] asks about the drug and the guy immediately insists it isn't just rebranded heroin.

Apparently it makes you feel strong enough to do anything.

Red Thunder is cooked in kitchen on the tier just above this one (tier 3?). The chef's name is [[Morgo]]. The guy we're talking to is named [[Orgo]]. No relation.

[[Keena O'Malley|Keena]] asks if Red Thunder makes his life better and Orgo goes on about how strong he is. But he looks hella weak and emaciated. 

He leaves to go find another ATM, and we agree to go visit this Morgo guy after we deal with the [[Malcolm Oublier|Malcolm]] situation. After seeing him, Kat tries to justify her thievery by saying the Cael Foundation shouldn't be setting up ATMs where people are just going to get money for drugs, which the rest of the party tries to explain doesn't work that way.

We discuss what else we need to do and how we should deal with our two captives in the trunk.

[[Jack Mercer|Jack]] comes over and says he'll pay us for our contributions to his video.

He offers to take care of our "trunk problem." Keena asks what he'll do and he says he'll repay them for robbing him earlier. Kat says he can take them, but she doesn't want to know anything about it.

We go back inside, where [[Dorian Caldwell|Dorian]] wants to look for the game cartridge. Kat notices that the [[Nyx|tiefling]] we saw before slyly went and took the cartridge and presumably still has it.

We chat briefly about what should be done with the game and Dorian floats using it almost like a trap/prison for defeated enemies. The party brings up some ethical concerns potential "prisoners" dying while trying to escape the game.

We talk to the tiefling, whose name is [[Nyx]]. She's looking for her brother, [[Styx]].

Styx had bought a set of cursed games and then disappeared sometime after saying he was starting one of them. However, by the time Nyx found out and went to his house, it had been looted, so she doesn't know which of the 6 Hamlet Keeper games he was playing. 

This was the first game she got her hands on. Nyx knew what would happen when the game was played and brought others into it anyway. She got a bunch of nerds together for the cursed playthrough thinking it would require a lot of video game skills, which backfired when it turned out your real life combat prowess matters more in the cursed game.

We offer to help so she doesn't get more innocent people trapped in the game, and ask for the cursed copy of the OG Hamlet Keeper so we can keep it from falling into the wrong hands (or for Dorian more as a souvenir). Nyx says she can't do that because she needs to sell this one to pay for the next cursed game she can track down, and mentions she'll still need to test this one again now to see if it's even still cursed after being beaten. We offer help with that as well.

She does let us know that time seemed to be frozen for people trapped in the game - no one aged or needed to eat or drink.

Dorian strikes a deal for the current game - he gives her money and a couple expensive bottles of wine with the promise of more later on after we determine if this copy is still cursed. That's a later problem though since we're currently in no shape to fight.

Kat scopes out the bedrooms and they're in good enough shape to provide comfortable rest.

Dorian asks which Hamlet Keeper game is the best, and Max remembers Hamlet Keeper 3 being considered the peak.

Jack returns and it becomes immediately obvious that he just killed the captives, much to Kat, Keena and Max's distress. Kat and Keena realize they were being willfully ignorant about the situation, 

We ask Jack about tracking down Hamlet Keeper 3 and he says he was in fact planning a series on all of the cursed games, so he might be able to help us find it.

Max and Kat talk morality on watch and confirm they're on the same page about not killing people, and that it was a bad idea to turn our captives over to Jack. Max talks about how he thinks most of these low-level guys are pawns and products of their circumstances, and that the real evil is the people at the top. Kat agrees the people at the top are bad, but is more hesitant to empathize with the "pawns", since she grew up in similar circumstances and chose differently. She also expresses that she's still worried about this whole Enforcers/courts thing backfiring and getting [[Kieran Graves|Kieran]]'s family killed.

Kat notices some Saints heading our way early enough to at least wake the others, but she and Max dawdle just a little too long talking to avoid combat entirely. The Saints spot us, pointing at Kat and yell that we're the ones that have been killing their boys.

![[session11_combat1.webp]]

Fortunately we subdue them with minimal injury and call the [[Enforcers]]. 

After realizing this will be another trip to court to testify, Kat asks Max to try and film combats from now on so we can always have evidence.

During the night, Keena had another vision.

![[Keena O'Malley#Betrayer Vision]]


She doesn't share with the group.

At the edge of an overlook on our way to the [[Ransom Location]], we see a bunch of people gathered around a stall showing a bunch of animated paintings.

![[session11_map2.webp]]

We see [[Animator|this guy]] standing behind the stall.

![[npc_animator.webp]]

"I have the magic touch! I can bring the pictures to life! Remember your loved ones and pets in living detail. With enough money and enough resources, you can even speak with them."

Keena asks Dorian if he thinks this guy could help him reconnect with his parents. He isn't sure yet. The vibe is giving conman but the pitch is intriguing.

Keena suddenly spots the [[Beggar from Keena's Vision|old man]] from her vision. He's panhandling in a gutter, covered in various fluids. He appears to be blind and has no tongue, leaving him unable to write or speak. Kat and Max both give him coins.

Dorian is interested enough that we decide to go over to the stall and learn more.

The crowd is all focused on the merchant and doesn't seem to notice the old man in the gutter. Keena tries to hang back discreetly to talk to him. She is in fact completely indiscreet, but we all assume she's going to do something nice for him and leave her to it, heading to the stall without suspicion.

Dorian asks if the animator is able to animate existing works and he says he can.

It costs 10 platinum for basic animation, but more advanced works take more time and effort.

He pulls out a painting of himself that talks to us as a demonstration. It talks and acts exactly like the real man.

He calls it an imprint of a person and says the more info is provided about the subject, the more true to life the depiction will be. Dorian muses that he doesn't know much about his parents, but the merchant says he has time to find it. It seems advanced works also require gathering a lot of materials.

Dorian asks the animator how he gets information on the subjects of the paintings and he says it's pretty much up to what the customer is able to provide.

Dorian agrees to pay for the initial basic animation, and potentially return for more once he has the funds and more information about his family. The guy pulls out a magic paintbrush. It repairs the painting easily and then he asks what kind of motion Dorian wants for the animation.

Dorian asks for just contented positive smiling. The animator obliges, and when he's handed the painting Dorian makes eye contact with his parents for the first time in many years.

He looks for a short moment, and then rolls the painting up and tucks it away for now.

Meanwhile, Keena is left alone with the beggar. 

She gives him a coin and asks if she can ask him some yes/no questions. He nods.

![[Beggar from Keena's Vision#Keena's Interrogation]]

She watches him shuffle away down a dark, lonely, and suspicious alley.

When we return from the stall, Keena tells us about how she had a not good vision about the beggar. The moment she says the vision told her to do something bad to him, Max walks away.

Keena and Kat discuss the situation and Kat is relieved to confirm Keena doesn't want to kill the old man, at least not without any evidence that he's done something to deserve it. They try to think of a better way to communicate with him but don't come up with anything. Max sees the [[Mystery Burger Man]]  He basically indicates that Max should follow him and dips. Max follows and finds only the bag of burgers with a note that says "enjoy."

Max finds no trace of the man, so he takes the bag and gives each of us a chili cheese burger without eating one himself. They are delicious.

As Keena and Max are talking about...something...We see a Titan Corp aerostatic drone lands near the ransom spot.

A team of Titan Corp goons emerge and swarm around [[Brug]] and tackle him to the ground.

Kat tells Max to start recording and he does. She wants to interfere, but it's too many guys for us to take on, especially in our current condition.

Meanwhile, Dorian asks Keena again about the old man. 

She explains everything she asked and his responses.

Dorian wonders if he could have been punished for going against the [[Oligarchs]].

The Titan goons have taken Brug and are now looking at a damaged engine on their transport. 

Kat floats the idea of trying to just talk to them. The party is hesitant about something going wrong and pulling us into combat, but she feels like she can at least play the curious onlooker and maybe get some information without having to lie.

![[session11_map3.webp]]

She walks up, looking at the smoking engine, and chats with one of the guys guarding the perimeter. He tries to recruit her and she plays along to keep the conversation, but gives him a random number (which turns out to be Max's) to send an application link.

As they chat, she finds out they will turn Brug into the court system on the accusation of terrorism for what he and the Box Boys did at the [[Pumping Station]]. Unsuccessful in talking her way into the secured area, she goes on her way and returns to the party.

We move on and make our way to the [[Ransom Location]].

It's a cargo warehouse. Seemingly abandoned, but we see quite a few [[Bellanova Family|Bellanovas]] hanging around outside. 

As we approach, we get a text that says, "send the chosen in." Kat says "send Malcolm out" but there's no reply to that or any additional texts.

Kat uses elementalism to summon a bong cloud around Max to hype him up. Kat wants to try and go with Max, but doesn't want to leave Keena and Dorian alone in their current condition either. Max enters alone.

![[location_bbmegatower_tier2_ransomlocation.webp]]

One of the large guards in the room tells Max needs to get in the box. Max tries to bargain for some leeway but big guy is getting paid to enforce this. Max is friendly and asks for his name and he refuses to give it. Max says a fake name is fine too and he says Max can call him [[Stu]].

![[npc_stu.webp|615]]

Max offers Stu a hug before he goes in the box and Stu accepts, saying to never pass up a free hug.

Max asks about Malcolm.

Stu shows a screen inside that he says will show Malcolm being released nearby once he goes in.

As Max protests about needing more certainty, the tv flicks on and shows Malcolm hooded and bound in what appears to be a van.

Max makes his case, and the van stops. The doors open and you can see it's somewhere in the megatower. A guy wearing a balaclava listens to orders through an earpiece and drags Malcolm to the edge of the door, unties him, and throws him out of the van.

Max asks if they'll drive off and leave him alone, but balaclava man doesn't move.

Stu says it's time to go in the box. Max acquiesces. Only then as the door shuts behind him does he see the van drive off, leaving Malcolm unconscious in the road.

The screen flips to show a man covered in bandages.

A voice comes from just off camera.

"So, Max, we meet again."

The voice says they'll meet in person soon as some kind of gas starts to flood the container and Max passes out.
