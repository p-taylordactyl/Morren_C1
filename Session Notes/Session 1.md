# Winter 1, 2012

## Initial Meeting

We start with [[Dorian Caldwell|Dorian]] at the [[Locations/Hood Station|Station House]].

![[location_hoodstation_stationhouse.webp]]

Other people start to trickle in - [[Silas Sorelli|Silas]], [[Gideon Oublier|Gideon]], [[Katriel Buchanon]], [[Maximilius Adkins|Max]], and [[Keena O'Malley|Keena]].

We all have personal communicators but they don't work without data cards (which provide unlimited for 30 days). Service can be limited in sketchy areas (ie deep underground). When we do have service, we have the ability to text, call, email, look things up online.

QuestApp (local quest board, pending rename) does not require service.

Right now, it's early in the morning on New Year's Day. 

The barkeep is an orcish man named [[Drogrash]]. 

We order breakfast.

Our QuestApps update and give us notifications. 

We get through initial introductions and decide to head out a-questing. The below are available to us, all with a 14 day expiration.

![[questboard_winter012021_morning.webp]]

After some discussion, we decide to head to the [[Not Far Sewer|sewer]] to tackle the first 3 quests on the board, then heading for the tower. Before we leave, we need to gear up, so we head to the market. Several of us were idiots and decided to take money instead of starting gear. No backpacks at the shop, so Max and Kat pay a stupid amount of money to commission some.

![[location_hoodstation_0.webp]]

Our initial shopping complete, we head to pick up our bikes and realize in the process that we purchased an idiotic amount of feed that currently takes up the entirety of our bike storage. Whoops.

![[location_hoodstation_endlinegarage.webp]]

We head out for the sewer. Around the [[Westreach]] area Kat decides to make some evasive manuevers (ie rolls a nat 1 on the navigation check) and it takes them twice the time it should have taken.

As we enter the sewer, Dorian finds a small satchel with some gold in it under a pile of rubble in the sewer.

Silas remembers the sewer is known as the "Not-Far Sewer" which is inhabited by both weirdos and toxic slime.

The sewer has two paths, one leading west and the other south. Silas vaguely remembers there being a bazaar to the south, and a pumping station off to the west.

We walk through the sewer for 4 hours and finally get within sight of the bazaar, as well as a mysterious door with a forcefield around it.

![[session1_map1.webp]]

We set up camp for the night. Gideon and Keena are on watch.

Keena tries to feel Gideon out and realizes quickly that he is very naive about the world, and adjusts her approach accordingly. She starts to talk to him more like a child as she gently but firmly explains that he was somewhat rude to her. He is ultimately receptive even if it seems like he doesn't quite understand.

While Gideon and Keena are chatting about their moms and taking care of family, he notices there's a forcefield around the door up to the right.

We see the bazaar on the way (red marker).

Max tries to zoom off towards the bazaar on his bike and wipes out. We hear a "splorching" noise and see slime, sliming at us aggressively.


![[location_notfarsewer_slimefight.webp]]

We defeat the slimes.

Keena and Kat have a conversation about dealing with Gideon.

![[session1_map2.webp]]

We roll up to the bazaar.

![[location_notfarsewer_bazaar.webp]]

The shopkeeper is a goblin named "[[Sprocket]]?" tells us there's a bunch of people that worship the water filter behind the forcefielded door. Recently they were saying the Machine God is sick. Sounds like one of our missions.

Keena reveals that Morrenel speaks to her, and that he's worried about heathen fish people, and if we run into heathens that we probably won't be able to change their minds so then we'll have to kill them because Morronel simply won't tolerate heresy. This sparks...a discussion.
