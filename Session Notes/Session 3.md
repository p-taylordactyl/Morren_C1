Max gets stung by a sewer bee and vanishes.

### Sewer Day 3 Watch

Silas and Dorian are on watch. Dorian peeks into a weird pipe with a fairly wide opening, and realizes there's something lodged within. He pulls it out and finds that it's a large plastic garbage bag. He cuts it open with his dagger, and a bunch of garbage falls out, along with a humanoid body wearing a costume/suit. Some kind of Costumed Freak.

![[npc_lumenveil.webp]]

Silas cuts open the costume and it self-heals. Silas realizes this guy is a hero with the [[Cael Foundation]] named [[Lumen Veil]]. Lumen doesn't really have powers but uses a specialized suit and weaponry made of a material called "Luminum." The suit is ridiculously expensive but DNA coded to the individual in a way that's very difficult to bypass.

Lumen gasps, he is alive. Silas asks what he was doing in the pipe but he can barely move. With Max bee-ed, we have no healing to offer. Lumen asks why he isn't dead but Silas doesn't know. He asks about our affiliations.

Silas says we're sort of roundabout friends of the Cael Foundation. Otherwise we would have killed him and taken his suit. Lumen is suspicious but says he was fighting some "undesirables" in the [[The Bellanova Family|Bellanova]] Towers. He was winning but then something hit him and he blacked out. He's really surprised he's not dead. Silas tries to check the suit, but no sign of damage. He would need to see Lumen's actual body. 

Lumen explains the suit will explode if he's killed.

Silas tells him we're going to Pipe Junction and Lumen says he can't be seen there. He offers to make it worth our while if we can heal him.

Unfortunately we really do have nothing.

Dorian offers to sell him a bike?!?!

Lumen asks about a communicator but we have no minutes.

Lumen is very concerned about protecting his secret identity and Silas says we've already seen his face. He offers that Lumen stick with us but he says he can't. The folks at Pipe Junction will recognize both his costumed and secret identity.

Silas directs him to the Sludge Farm and Bazaar for assistance.

They fill him in a bit about Hood Station and he offers to stop by and visit. They tell him to keep the info about us on the down low and he's agreeable. They help him use a pipe as a crutch, and he hobbles off towards the Sludge Farm.
