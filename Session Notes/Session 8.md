# Winter 9, 2012

The lady in the [[Penthouse|penthouse]] is watching TV on a projector outside and doesn't immediately notice us.

Our quest definitely implied that the penthouse was long abandoned and it definitely isn't. This place is super bougie and she looks rich.

![[session8_penthousemap.webp]]

Since the lady is outside, we carefully sneak into the apartment, noting along the way that we are being picked up on cameras. Whoops. After poking around we come across a massive computer. Not super new or sleek looking. 

Kat hacks into the computer. It's set up as guest terminal that lets whoever's currently in the penthouse use it. The guest account looks like it gets wiped after each stay. There's an admin account as well, but the security is stronger and Kat can't get in.

The guest account does have access to the security cameras, which at least on this machine are not live feeds and instead just record. Kat turns off the cameras and scrubs the footage of our entry. Then we start looking through the files on the computer to see who's currently staying here.

The individual watching movies outside is named [[Elyra Sol]]. The info about her background is intentionally vague and none of us recognize the name. We find a lot of video-editing software, random files of actors we recognize and a performance that was seemingly filmed in this location. Lots of tasteful art porn, but we also recognize the [[The Troupe|Troupe]] performance identified in [[Penthouse Signal Rehearsal]].

[[Silas Sorelli|Silas]] is able to find an empty 32gb data stick in a drawer, and we copy the Troupe related files to that. and then Kat wipes the entire user profile as if the software glitched and reset it back.

As the PC reboots, we notice a logo in the corner of the login screen. It shows what appears to be Sky City in symbolic form with a little A in the corner. It seems familiar to Dorian but he can't quite place it.

Our work done, we start making our way out. Elyra has moved to the living room but is still watching TV and we don't have any trouble avoiding her. 

[[Dorian Caldwell|Dorian]] does have us stop in the kitchen so he can steal a bottle out of the wine cabinet. Kat looks around the kitchen for any other goods but doesn't find much (or maybe just money - this is me filling in a week later and I don't super remember).

We make it back to the elevator without incident and try to figure out our next move to complete our last ongoing quest, [[The Silent Floor]]. We're pretty sure we need to head east, but don't know much more than that. It seems like even though the restricted tiers the elevator stops at aren't directly where we need to go, they might be able to get us there. 


![[session8_map2.webp]]

Kat tries and fails to hack the top level tier. Maybe the weird little bobblehead isn't so lucky after all.

We go down to Tier 2, the only one she hasn't tried yet. Again Kat can't manage to hack in. Silas offers to cast knock, and after weighing the risk and our options we decide to do it.

There's an arcane lock on the door, so it will relock after a few minutes. We go in.

The area still seems pretty bad, just less bad than the lower floors and more locked down.

Lots of probable drug dens. Some residential areas, some industrial. Little watchtowers with [[The Bellanova Family|Bellanova]] snipers on each corner. Notably we aren't the only folks keeping our faces covered around here.

We find an alley to park the car and get some rest. [[Gideon Oublier|Gideon]] and Silas are on watch.  They talk about costumes and being vigilantes.  Silas thinks it's all just different flavors of thug, and it's better to have at least some good people doing it. Gideon says the last 24 hours have kind of messed him up, mentally.

Silas also talks to Gideon about drugs. He explains that many people who do drugs do so to fill gaping wounds in their soul. However, some people (like himself) don't have wounds like that and just do them for creativity and inspiration.

Gideon asks if he should try drugs to help him be more creative the way his mom wants. Silas encourages him to not worry about trying to use magic the way that works for Morrgen, and instead to find his own path and methods that work for him.

Gideon's wrist phone goes off with a notification.

A content creator he follows just dropped a social media post. It's [[Jack Mercer]], a collectathon hunter. Collects rare media and comic books. Says he's in the [[BB Megatower]] getting content for a new video on a legendary game called Hamlet Keeper. Supposedly there's a legendary cursed copy around where everyone who's ever played it has disappeared. Weirdly it seems like he's actually on our street.

Gideon asks Silas if he knows about Mercer and about the game. Silas does. 

Gideon thinks the cursed game might be tied to the missing floors of the building - like if enough people watched the game being played at once. Silas doesn't seem to buy it but humors him.

After realizing that Mercer was close enough for Gideon to be in the background of a livestream earlier, we decide to try and find him to talk to him.

We locate him fairly quickly but he's in bad shape. It looks like he's been robbed and beaten.

![[npc_jackmercer.webp|465]]

We don't have any health potions, but Kat successfully punches his dislocated shoulder back into place.

Gideon offers that we can give him a ride and look for the thieves.

Jack asks for Gideon's name and they talk about how he needs a good superhero name. Jack says if we get some clout the Cael Foundation will notice us. Gideon asks for a selfie to help with the clout thing.

Jack would and post it on his own account but we need to get his phone back first.

Gideon asks if he found the game. Jack says he was close. He made a device to track the specific frequency the old era console used - it's somewhere in the eastern section of the megatower but then he was robbed. If we get it back he could find it for sure.

The missing bag with the device is a brown satchel with the channel logo (silhouette of Jack's face).

Jack says it's possible that the game is related to our quest. Places where people played the game tend to go quiet.

He tells us he didn't see the thieves at all but could hear that they left on foot. Silas is able to pick up their trail.

Jack hides in the trunk of the car while we follow the tracks.

As we discuss the ethics of murder, we see a group of [[The West Valence Saints|Saints]] beating the shit out of the grappling hook kid we saw before. Another priest, another scarred guy, and several lower level types. They toss his package over the edge. 

They push one of their own forward, telling him to finish the job. The [[Kieran Graves|kid]] looks to be 18 and is very hesitant. 

![[npc_kieran.webp]]

Kat steps out immediately and tells him he doesn't have to do this. She says that she didn't and she made it out. The priest calls her a traitor, but Kat's words were enough to keep the kid from going through with it. The Saints attack and the kid fights with us.

Annoyingly, I somehow didn't grab a screenshot of the battle.

We manage to take out the majority of the group nonlethally, the exception being one of the grunts, whose head got blown off when Gideon accidentally supercharged his sorcerous burst damage while thinking about how annoying Malcolm is.

We talk to the kid and the grappling hook guy.

The grappling hook guy is named [[Jax Riven]]. He's a courier in the tower who specializes in going through the collapsed sections. He doesn't ask questions about the packages, just delivers them. 

Gideon asks if he ever brought something to a floor and then the floor disappeared. Jax says he did, actually.

He doesn't have time to take us there, but one of the missing floors is technically outside in the southeastern section. We can get there from this floor by following the corridor all the way. Jax then drops down below to retrieve his package before heading off to make the delivery.

Then we talk to the kid. His name is Kieran Graves (didn't get the last name written down). He was working for the Saints to provide for his family, who are all still in [[Westreach]]. 

This sect is called the Lightning. Kind of an imitator of the Dominion Crew, whose violent ideology is starting to spread.

We try to decide what to do with the knocked out guys. Kieran is super worried about leaving any alive because his family is still in [[Westreach]] and if the Saints find out what he did, they'll kill everyone. 

Kat really doesn't want to kill anyone especially when they're knocked out but at the same time, she's having trouble figuring out any other way to keep this from coming back on Kieran's family.

As she's about to make the call and let Dorian take care of it, Jax mentions they can just turn these guys into the [[Oligarchs]]. He gives us a number to call that will send a team of enforcers to come pick them up and take them to jail, kind of a citizens arrest type thing. However, without evidence of wrongdoing we may need to testify in court. 

Kat is worried this won't really protect Kieran's family if the Saints are able to contact the main crew, but he says he thinks it will buy enough time to get his family out if they have somewhere to go. Kat invites him to Hood Station though she's very light on details and doesn't give the actual location yet. They trade numbers and Kat says she'll give him a meetup location soon (not the Station itself but somewhere in Cloud District so she can meet the family before bringing them in).

We call the Oligarchs line to pick up the bodies and they say that without video evidence someone will need to testify against them. We agree to it though Kat gets very worried about it how this could come back to bite them or Kieran's family. Silas and Dorian are down to kill everyone but will let Kat call this one.

She feels like the smart choice is to kill them but can't bring herself to do it, and with the enforcers on their way there's no time anyway. Dorian tosses the guy Gideon killed into the pit, and we decide to take the priest and another Saint hostage so we can find out what all they know about Kat and if they've gotten anything from our [[Stalker]].

We find Jack's bag as well, and then hightail it out of there with our prisoners just before the enforcers roll up. They look like this.

![[misc_enforcer.webp|509]]

We get back to the car and let Jack out of the trunk and give him his bag back. His device is still working and he says we need to keep heading down the corridor, which lines up with what Jax told us.

We pile back into the car, with Jack having to sit on one the lap of one of our unconscious Saints. 

As we're driving (with Dorian at the wheel) a gnome jumps out in front of the car and Dorian narrowly avoids hitting him.

![[npc_paintbrushgnome.webp|703]]

The gnome (not sure if we got a name?) tells us a guy named [[Bron]] that runs the [[Neon Corridor]] stole his magic paintbrush and he wants help getting it back. Dorian is interested in this paintbrush and agrees to keep an eye out. The gnome vanishes and says we'll never see him again unless we get the paintbrush. Weird little guy.

We carry on and soon see the Neon Corridor the gnome mentioned - it's a little out of the way, but not much, and Dorian really wants to go. The rest of the group wants to stay focused and stop by on the way back, but we compromise by agreeing to check it out but keep it brief - no venturing too deep into the area.

![[session8_map3.webp]]


As we enter the corridor, a giant lizard runs full speed at Silas and Kat, both of whom stay still as it approaches. It stops short and licks each of their faces before running away and halfway up a wall.

![[location_bbmegatower_neoncorridor.webp]]

A bunch of people down the street are throwing bottles at the wall. Kat throws bottles and cheers with them and an Orc comes over, compliments her throwing, and introduces himself as Bron.

![[npc_Bron.webp|760]]

Kat ends up giving Bron her racist and decidedly unlucky ogre bobblehead, which she was very worried about bc Gideon acted like this guy was an ogre, but then he thinks the bobblehead is hilarious. He might be racist. 

It turns out Bron runs some kind of pawn shop. His story is that the gnome sold him the paintbrush fair and square and he's already sold it to someone else. Which hasn't stopped the gnome from continually convincing people to come looking for it. Bron's clearly pretty annoyed about it.

I think we got some info on who he sold it too but can't remember for sure.

We leave the Neon Corridor and keep heading East/Southeast. 

Somewhere in here (or maybe before the neon corridor) is a conversation about how we want to interrogate the prisoners (which we've decided to leave for closer to our destination). Kat thinks she shouldn't be involved unless it's to make one of the others look stronger/tougher.

Kat and Gideon are on watch. During the first part, Malcolm texts Gideon from a casino and says it's so easy to make money. Lol. Kat says he's such a twerp.

Gideon and Kat chat about murder and how Gideon isn't messed up about killing someone the way Kat was. Kat talks about how she feels like it's really important to have a line you aren't willing to cross. Because once you let yourself justify crossing it once, the justifications come easier until suddenly the line is fuzzy, and eventually starts to move. 

Gideon gets a text from the stalker showing the outside of the casino where Malcolm is.

He texts Malcolm warning him.

Another text from the stalker. "Who will you give for him?" along with pictures of Keena and Max, who are both still bee-ed to our knowledge.

Kat tells Gideon to reply they can have her but before he can even type another text comes in that says "We don't want her." Clearly they're listening to us somehow.

The party discusses what might be going on and who has a better chance of surviving whatever's happening here (Keena) but we end the session without making a final decision or replying to the texts.
