# Winter 5, 2012

> [!important]
> Special thanks to Cinna for generously sharing her notes - this is pretty much a direct copy paste with updates to some links.

Kat and Max are stung by dimensional sewer bees.

[[Dorian Caldwell|Dorian]] and [[Keena O'Malley|Keena]] each whack one of the sleeping gators, almost killing them but not quite. [[Silas Sorelli|Silas]] then tries to obscure the gator near Dorian using [[silent-image-xphb|Silent Image]] to create an inky darkness. [[Gideon Oublier|Gideon]] fries the gator in front of Keena, and then Dorian kills the last gator.

The group asks [[Sister Hera]] to fix the filter -- she says they have tried, the clergy has done everything to fix the filter that they can by replacing mechanical parts and such and that the filter is just depressed. Keena insight checks Hera and realizes she is not bullshitting them, and Silas recognizes there is nothing wrong with the machine on a mechanical level. Keena doesn't feel any divine magic, and Gideon knows there's just regular ambient arcane magic. The system turns on but nothing functionally happens. Silas tries to go through the previous command history.

Dorian threatens the machine to start working saying he'll both dynamite, which turns the filter on and it starts pumping water again. Sister Hera recognizes it's terrible but is appreciative it's worked.

Keena asks Sister Hera more about the [[Machine God]] and the clergy and the other religious sites. Sister Hera says the cult is very separated but realizes there may be another "sick" machine somewhere else in the system. She also believes that machines were made to serve the living but it's a cyclical nature and the living serve machines too. She tries to say the group has witnessed a miracle, but Gideon and Keena know there was no divine magic. Sister Hera gives Dorian 100 gold for fixing the machine, which they split. The group heads back to the [[Floodgate Bazaar]] and [[Sprocket]] tells them he has new stuff in stock.

The group heads down toward the creepy whispering pipe area, and it's Gideon and Keena on watch. They talk about the heretical cult, and Keena is a bit worried they won't make it to the tower in time with how long it's taken them to run around the [[Not Far Sewer]] so far and how much they have left to do before their missions are due. Suddenly, a smell of death (both ancient and recent) comes from the culvert. In his sleep, Silas lights his incense. Keena falls, knocks her head, and becomes unconscious.

Keena then has a [[Keena O'Malley#Candle Vision|vision]]: 
![[Keena O'Malley#Candle Vision]]

The group goes down to the culvert, where they see some creepy, macabre individuals. There's one tiefling that picks up a skeleton and starts snapping the bones to wear: a jawbone as kneepads, part of the skull as a hat.

The group heads toward the [[Bone Culvert]] and toward the people, and one guy just starts barking at them and warns them away from his bone pile. Keena tries to get her name, and she says names are dangerous things but finally gives it ([[Vesh Hollowbone]]). When she asks what they're doing, Gideon says they're there for the "children". Vesh recommends that they not say that, but he does it again, and she and the group leave. Vesh tells them they should follow -- they do.

They watch as two pairs of undead (adult skeletons and zombies) come out of the water, look around, scream, and go back in the water. She tells the group the dead show up when things are said they don't like: most recently, talks of children.

Vesh says collects things that she needs, which most people down in the sewer do, and that she was born in the sewer and will most likely die in the sewer. Gideon and Keena convince Vesh to help them kill the dead and cleanse the culvert.

They head back down, piss off the undead, and then a blue orb zooms out, then the bones outside of the water rise up into a bone pillar. First to 3 points wins, via being inside the ritual activation site, after which it will move. The undead can just touch it, but the living have to get there with religious knowledge.

![[location_notfarsewer_boneculvert_combat.webp]]

### Round 1

On either end of the waterway is an enemy reinforcement zone. Vesh helps the group get a point, and Silas uses [[silent-image-xphb|Silent Image]] to create the vision of a wall to force zombies to cut off their LOS and move around it. At the end of round 1, the Living have 1 point and the Undead have 0 points.

### Round 2

Gideon tries to fry the zombie in the waterway, to good effect. The melee zombie does big damage to Keena's temp HP (false life). The one zombie runs over and activates the ritual site. It moves, and Vesh is able to activate it. Dorian and Keena kill a zombie. Silas drops concentration on the image and and dodges. One zombie hits Another zombie activates the ritual site.

Living: 2
Undead: 2

### Round 3

The bone pillar and activation site appear right under Keena and Gideon. The skeletons kill [[Vesh Hollowbone|Vesh]]. Gideon and Dorian try to activate the ritual site to no affect. Keena tries, fails, uses a hero point and fails again, then with human inspiration and the power of Sewer Cat (Ruby), succeeds. The Bone pillar disappears, as does the orb and with it, the smell of ancient death.

Living: 3
Undead: 2

Keena gives Gideon a health potion, gives Vesh the rites of Morrenel, and consoles Gideon with the facts of life and the Circle of Life. A centipede tries to eat her and gets a chunk out before Gideon (and Keena) runs her off. Gideon loads her onto a motorcycle, and Keena tries to respect the fact this is probably his first death but moves away while Silas and Dorian tell him he can't take a body with them because it's gonna start smelling and shit.

They bury her body well under bones, so she can't (easily) be eaten by centipedes and shit. After an hour, they start to make their way through the tunnel. which seems to be more like catacombs, and as they walk by, they see 7 urchin-like child dolls with candles in front of them. Keena takes up the candles.

The dolls don't have any sense of religious or cult magic when Gideon checks, but he could them open to check further (he doesn't). They pick up the dolls and throw them on the motorcycles.

Dorian just opens one, and beating organs (which were placed anatomically correct) fall out, stopping beating as they hit the floor. Gideon is distressed. The group decides to continue moving forward.

They come across a cistern with many shelves going up, filled with more dolls but these are all adults (saints, office workers, etc.). The ones higher up are older, and there are no children beyond the ones we have. Upon investigation, there is no dust disturbances along the floors or anything walking around. There are dust disturbances where the dolls were placed but not much more. None of the dolls in the cistern have candles.

Gideon gets stung with bees, and the group leaves the cistern and heads to the [[Pumping Station]]. They take a three man watch, and Keena tells Dorian and Silas about her dream, saying they need to find the houses of the wicked (most likely those who stole/killed the children) and then lighting the candles will return the lost children.

Keena tries to go to sleep to see if she'll have another vision, and Gideon doesn't want to take another watch so he goes to sleep too.

We wake up the next day and approach [[Kelm]] working on the fucked up pipe -- he is flanked by two constructs. 

![[npc_kelm.webp|312]]

All of them are clearly part of the [[Titan Corporation]]. Every time he tries to divert the water, they get menacingly close to the guy and force the water to other pumps. He tells them to fix the blockage, the constructs won't attack them if they don't attack them, and the constructs won't interfere if they try to fix the blockage.

![[location_notfarsewer_pumpingstation.webp]]

The collapsed water way drains a bit, and they see a ladder. Kelm warns Keena about moving orange caustic goo at the bottom, and then they jump down.

![[location_notfarsewer_pumpingstation_blockage.webp]]
