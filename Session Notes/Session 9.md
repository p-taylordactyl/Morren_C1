#  Winter 11, 2012

We resume on the 2nd tier of the [[BB Megatower]], following Jack Mercer's device as it leads us toward the silent/dark floors.

![[session9_map1.webp]]

[[Gideon Oublier|Gideon]] gets bee-ed out as [[Keena O'Malley|Keena]] and [[Maximilius Adkins|Max]] are suddenly bee-ed back in. We fill them in on everything they missed.

[[Katriel Buchanon|Kat]] says her phone number out loud for the [[Stalker|stalker]] to text her in lieu of Gideon. [[Dorian Caldwell|Dorian]] , but he and Kat both get texts. While it seems the stalker is somehow able to listen to us in real time (based on prev response to Kat talking out loud) they also have a lot of information on our party as a whole.

"The time will come. We will meet. We will trade. Decide by then."

![[session9_map2.webp]]


We weight the merits of Keena vs Max, each of whom thinks it should be them, but don't come to a decision.

Instead, we decide it's time to start figuring out who our stalker is and start looking for phone relay to try and hack the signal/track the phone they use to communicate.

We find the relay surprisingly quickly - it's surrounded by what appear to be Machine God worshippers with little makeshift shrines. Dorian tries to find a way we might be able to sneak in, but we don't have much luck.

The worshippers pay us no mind as we walk straight up to the door. There are two levers. Kat checks them out with her tinkers tools. One lever seems to be malfunctioning and will blow up the place, the other just opens the door. 

Kat pulls the lever that won't blow everyone up and we enter the [[Relay Shrine]]. Dorian leaves a sign on the other lever saying not to pull it.

![[location_bbmegatower_relayshrine.webp]]

![[npc_shrinepriest1.webp]]

![[npc_shrinepriest2.webp]]

These two guys will get the Machine Spirit inside the machine to help us figure out who owns the phone number of our stalker. It requires a donation. Kat and Dorian each kick in 2 platinum.

The guy will soothe the machine spirit and his partner will make technical commands.

Dorian gives them his phone and clarifies we're looking for information on the redacted number.

This is going to take a few hours. The give us some sacred water - runoff from the Machine. It tastes shockingly clean. Keena tries to detect any divine magic but gets nothing.

Kid comes in. Tech people are named [[Arden]] and [[Dex Dad]]?

![[npc_dex.webp]]

This kid is named [[Dex]] and he's a big fan of Max's content.

Max shows him the footage he got of the orc shoving ball bearings in his mouth.

Dex is something of a classic Gen Z/Gen Alpha kid who wants Max to make more content like this and less stuff about the government. 

Max is clearly bothered by this but tries to be polite about it. [[Silas Sorelli|Silas]] creates an illusion of some brainrot fruit for Dex to watch while Max talks to help him focus for more than 7 seconds at a time. It actually helps a lot.

[[Jack Mercer|Jack]] starts roasting Max for his content on the brainrot end of the spectrum. It seems to get under Max's skin a bit but he walks away before things get too heated.

The priests let us know they're finally breaking through to the phone number.

The number is from a corporate series - a fleet account from Titan Corporation. They have really good encryption. 

No matter who's using it, it always registers as Titan Corp - no individual name attached.

Keena asks if we can get a more specific location, but to do we would need telemetry at 3 different points around the city at the time when a message comes in. Would require a lot more time and coordination.

Dorian tries to send a text to see if we can get more info but it doesn't go through - the receiving device is off. 

Their cloud or cell might have a machine spirit of its own that could be communicated with but likely it'd be somewhere in a Titan Corpo's possession.

Dex almost pulls the explosion lever and the party just barely stops him. They tell the priests about how it could explode everyone and one goes to fix it.

Kat asks some questions about the Machine God.

Either Arden or Faile (unsure) talks about how Morronel isn't more powerful than the other gods, but rather just the creator of the contract who is equally bound by it.

They talk about morality of murder. The Machine God priest guy says he doesn't like to kill but would do it if pushed, especially in defense of others.

Silas and Kat have an intense convo about her reaction to killing people and his reaction to her guilt. Silas is very worried that Kat's guilt over killing even bad people is going to make her hesitate in a key moment and get him or someone else innocent killed. Kat argues she has to feel bad in order to know she's still good. 

Dex accidentally hits Keena in the head with a wrench and she falls unconscious. 

While she's out, she gets a divine message.

![[Keena O'Malley#Temple Instruction]]

Keena wakes up. 

She takes Dex aside and asks if he's aware of any wicked people in the room and he says absolutely not. She asks about recent converts and he says technically he's a recent convert, but no one else.

She asks who he worshipped before and he says nothing, until Faile?? (what is this guy's name) adopted him.

This kid reminds Keena of Gideon. 

She presses him trying to figure out who present may be wicked, but gets nowhere and returns to the group.

The priest comes back and says the explosion lever is fixed. Kat tests the repaired door lever and thank god it works. That was dicey for a moment.

She thanks the Machine God by doing an oil change on the car and pouring the old oil in the shrine (since the priest mentioned used oil/components are the preferred sacrifice).

![[session9_map3.webp]]

 With slightly more info to go on but still no communication from the stalker, we decide to keep heading towards the [[The Silent Floor|silent floor]]. We see a temporary dwelling, and on top of the building is graffiti that says "The [[Quiet Place]]."

Jack starts climbing a rusty pipe to get an establishing shot while Max starts filming too, but from the ground. Jack falls. Keena catches him, saving his life. He asks her to explain why she did it on camera. She basically says it was just the right thing to do. He also asks what the party wants to be called but we can't decide without Gideon.

Jack starts spieling about all the heroic things we've done for him for his viewers when suddenly, a portal appears and a [[Solomon Hexley|wizard]] steps out. His eyes glow blue as he points at Silas.

![[npc_solomonhexley.webp]]

He accuses Silas of stealing his spellbook. Silas tries to go invisible but the guy has See Invisibility. He wants 500 gold for the spellbook, which Silas refuses and Solomon says that he'll fireball us then.

![[location_bbmegatower_wizardfight.webp]]

Keena tries to offer to pay the 500 gold if we all go in but the party isn't on board.

It becomes clear we're not trying to kill each other and everyone is aiming to incapacitate. Dorian pulls out the bottle of wine (worth 100gp) he stole from the penthouse and offers it in lieu of gold, saying it's worth 700gp. Solomon is suspicious but accepts, for now.

Silas says he only stole the book because he couldn't afford it and Solomon says "you couldn't have just asked me?"

Silas says there was no other way but Solomon says that Silas could have asked him. He says they could have shared the book. He pulls a blank spellbook out and said he would have given it to Silas, but now he doesn't want to.

They have a civil conversation in which Solomon offers for them to help each other but Silas seems only marginally interested. Solomon leaves civilly, though he promises to return if the wine isn't legit. 

Kat didn't realize the wine was fake, and feels bad that they deceived him when he seems to be treating Silas quite fairly at the end.

We hear rattling in a vent. Kat hears a noise and it's a cog (symbol of the Machine God) rattling around in a vent. It seems attached to something. Kat pulls it out and finds a spiked tail of a manticore.

Our friends in the trunk are awake. Keena and Max will conduct the interrogation. They pull the acolyte out first.

Keena threw a burlap sack over her head to hide her identity and is playing the "good cop" of sorts while Max takes on the character of "The Tickler" and gives a performance so perfect it defies words. Anyway he stabs himself in the leg.

![[pc_maxadkins_thetickler.webp]]

The acolyte doesn't even know [[Kieran Graves|Kieran's]] name and only called him derogatory nicknames.

The leader of the [[Lightning Crew]] is named [[Lightning Leroy]].

The acolyte's name is [[Jerry]] and his specific area of influence is channeling Morronel. The Lightning Crew are trying to take the [[BB Megatower]] from the [[Bellanova Family|Bellanovas]]. 

They put the acolyte back in the trunk and pull out the grunt.

We interrogated the grunt and also did not get much from him.
