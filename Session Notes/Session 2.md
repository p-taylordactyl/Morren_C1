# Winter 2, 2012

We resume in the [[Not Far Sewer|Sewer]], midway through a somewhat contentious discussion of [[Keena O'Malley|Keena]]'s adamance that heathens probably need to be murdered.

We take a short rest and then approach the forcefield.

[[Silas Sorelli|Silas]] and some of the party try to see if the forcefield can be disabled, which turns up nothing. However we might have easier access at a "[[Pipe Junction]]." Unfortunately we have no idea where that is. [[Dorian Caldwell|Dorian]] looks for thieves cant signs and finds

`"home, shelter, supplies, reasonable, fanatic"

A debate begins on the most time-efficient way to proceed. Keena and [[Katriel Buchanon|Kat]] want to go back to the bazaar to talk to [[Sprocket]], since he would likely know both where Pipe Junction is and how to get there. However, the rest of the party don't want to backtrack and think it's better to explore and see if we can find someone around.

![[session2_map1.webp]]

It's eventually decided that we'll take the stairs and see if we can't find someone along the way.

As we're searching, [[Maximilius Adkins|Max]] and Kat spot what appears to be a tiefling hiding in a pipe, smoking a cigarette.

The tiefling doesn't want to come out. Max introduces himself. The tiefling doesn't want to give their name but says we can call them "[[Pipey]]." 

Pipey tells us Pipe Junction is an controlled by smugglers, and there are whispers that they're associated with the [[Bellanova Family|Bellanovas]].

Max asks about the forcefield. Pipey says it used to be open but has gotten less so since the cultists went into mourning.

Keena asks what the cult is about and Pipey says they preach machines having souls and a grand machine that ties it all together. They think the filter contains a spirit or part of the spirit of a grand machine.

All the else we get is that Pipe Junction is somewhere to the northeast.

We get to the top of the stairs and it's time to rest. Kat and Dorian are on watch together and chat a bit about their day jobs.

![[session2_map2.webp]]

# Winter 3, 2012

Time to set out, and we have more decisions to make. We'd like to get up to the top of the wall to the northeast, but the most direct route would require either a significant climb we aren't equipped for, or an incredibly dangerous bike jump.

There's also a point of interest off to the west, which Kat makes out as a mud "farm" of some kind, with some distant movement and toad-like figures.

After another extended discussion, we decide not to attempt the most direct route, and instead go around to try and find another way up. The mud area is on the way, so we'll check it out as we get closer.

We approach the mud pit and see that it is indeed a bountiful-looking farm, with humanoid figures working and toads sitting in the irrigation channels. There is notably a fungus that seems to permeate the water. 

Kat and Dorian sneak closer to get a better look.

![[location_notfarsewer_sludgefarm.webp]]

Kat can tell the water has a current but not where it comes from. The people are armed, but seem simple and very poor.

Dorian notes that they're creating a surplus crop. 

We try to sneak closer to steal some food, but Kat gets caught by a farmer. She initially pretends to be starving to avoid a fight.

Kat learns that this is the [[Sludge Farm]]. The farmer's name is [[Mira Sporehand]].

![[npc_mirasporehand.webp]]

She assures us the fungus situation is really due to the filter being bad and not a problem with the mushrooms themselves. They've been told so by someone called the "[[The Driftwarden]]"

The [[Cult of the Machine God|Machine Spirit people]] did at some point also send inquisitors to the sludge farm to deal with the filter problem. They agreed the issue is actually that the filter needs to be replaced.

We get clear on the problem but are undecided on the solution. We discuss the possibility of migrating the farm to Hood Station.

Gideon pushes Kat to reveal her past with the [[West Valence Saints|Saints]] to try and bond with Mira. This backfires as the mere mention of the gang sends the farmer into a panic of trying to placate us in any way possible. Kat repeatedly reassures her that she is no longer affiliated and that if anything, there are people in the gang who want to kill her. It kind of works? 

Keena asks Kat what she did for the gang.

We realize somehow these mushrooms are reproducing in some kind of unusual way.

After further discussion we realize this is all solved if we can divert the outflow that goes to Hood Station before it gets shroomed. This diversion can be done at Pipe Junction, which again, is controlled by smugglers.

Gideon asks about kids and Mira talks abouts hearing rumors of kids in the sewers.

Much debate on the best way to deal with the mushroom issue.

Gideon is able to convince the Mira and the [[Sludge Farmers]] to move the farm to Hood Station after the next harvest.
