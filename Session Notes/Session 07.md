# Winter 8, 2012

We resume in this area somewhere in the depths of the [[BB Megatower]].

![[session7_map1.webp]]

[[Malcolm Oublier|Malcolm]] just kicked Gideon in the nuts and tried to run, but Kat easily tackles him.

She doesn't let Gideon sorcerous burst his brother in the face at first. Malcolm keeps whining to be let go but when Kat loosens even a little he tries to get another nut shot on Gideon. 

Between back and forth ball-kicking and ball-sorcerous-bursting attempts, Kat and Gideon tell Malcolm that they're giving him a couple days to get his shit together before we step in and tell [[Morrgen Oublier|Morrgen]] what's going on. Gideon takes Malcolm's chain as collateral and explain to him how if he'd given some collateral to these guys he owes money too, maybe they wouldn't have needed to come after him.

Malcolm agrees to the terms and skedaddles as Gideon finally nails him with a burst.

We hear some clamor in the distance.

As we stand, a human in his 20s falls through the ceiling and slams into the ground in front of us. 

![[npc_jaxriven.webp]]

He says he has no time to talk, he has a delivery to make. He uses a grappling hook to anchor up to the shaft. Kat yells after him to ask where he got the hook and he says he got it at the [[Infinity Plaza Mall|Mall]].

We chat about the mall. Max loves cocaine and pretzels and both can be found at the mall, often together.

The area we're in is some sort of semi-commercial street. The street we're on now has a little restaurant called Krystal's that sells burgers/sliders.

We're lingering outside the burger shop and this [[Mystery Burger Man|this guy]] comes out with a bag of burgers.

![[npc_mysteryburgerman.webp|549]]

He says the burger will treat you how you treat it. 

Silas was bragging about his ability to demolish a burger. Burger Guy calls Silas the "Burger Destroyer" and Silas provides his actual name, which the orc says he'll remember. Silas asks the orc's name and the he doesn't answer, just starts housing burgers at an impressive clip as he walks away.

We go in and buy a metric fuckton of dirt cheap burgers. As in most of us purchase 1-2 dozen and then Dorian drops a platinum on the counter and buys 250 burgers.

Unfortunately it seems these sliders, though magnificent, don't keep for very long. Silas casts detect magic - nothing on the burgers, but significant transmutation magic in the back room of the restaurant. He says nothing of this to the party and simply starts wolfing burgers.

We have to share the massive tower of burgers we purchased, it's too many and we can't take them with us. The community comes together in the name of burger consumption.

We ask some of the people around if there's a good way to get to the elevator or some kind of access panel.

A random is a dick about it because there are literally signs. Silas takes the opportunity to again try and convince Kat to wield her background for intimidation purposes.

Max and Kat see a little drone in the background that seems to be watching them but it quickly zooms off. We can't tell who it belongs to.

Gideon gets a text from an unknown number with a video attachment. It's a video of two separate pairs (us) approaching a [[Box Boys|Box Boys]] Orc as well as a private conversation between the pairs off to the side, which the Box Boy never saw. The message says "I know what you did" followed by another clip of a headline about the Box Boys taking the [[Pumping Station]]. 

Another video comes in - footage of us in [[Pipe Junction]] dropping [[Prospera Bellanova]]'s name.

Another video of Max wolfing 30 burgers just now, which Max insists is AI because he only had a couple burgers.

Another picture. Someone in a hospital bed - completely wrapped in bandages, hard to tell who it is, along with the word "[[Augustin Devereaux|Devereaux]]." They almost look like a mummy and seem to be heavily supported by various braces and casts. This one confuses us all at first.

Kat asks Dorian what we should do. 

Max suddenly remembers the name Devereaux and realizes it's the guy he and Keena beat into a coma. He is light on details and describes it to the party as an accident where he and Keena were just interrogating the guy super hard about the accident he caused/covered up and somehow that led to coma.

We're discussing what to do about all of this and what the person texting us wants when a group of Saints enters the street, heading straight for us, including this creepy looking priest guy. Kat doesn't recognize any individuals.

![[session7_saints_spellcaster.webp|629]]

Their squad includes the above priest-looking guy and the [[Scarred Saint]]. Kat doesn't recognize anyone else. No time to talk, because they attack immediately. 

![[session7_saintscombat.webp]]

Silas wastes time of one guy with an illusion of [[Lumen Veil]].

Dorian one-shots the priest-y spellcaster.

Kat kills 3 people over the course of the combat. At the end she just keeps lashing at the last man she killed with her elemental chains until Gideon pulls her off.

A crowd is starting to form and people are filming. We need to go. As we look for somewhere to hide out, Silas checks the corpse of the acolyte and takes his symbol of Morronel.

We go up to the mysterious door in the top right corner. It appears to just be a residence, with a sign that says "No Soliciting." Silas puts up an illusionary wall blocking us from the view of the crowd below. 

Kat starts having a panic attack on the doorstep about having killed 3 people.

This orc comes out and asks what we need. 

![[npc_residentialorc.webp|507]]


Gideon says there was a kerfuffle and asks if we can hide out until it calms down.

The orc looks at Kat and beckons us in.

The orc did see the fight and says we shouldn't talk but is giving us a moment to catch our breath. 

Inside the dwelling, the party tries to help Kat come to terms with what happened. Gideon keeps trying to convince her that she's remembering wrong, no one really died and it's fine. Silas says he doesn't understand the fuss - it was self defense and they had it coming. Kat says "they had it coming" sounds exactly like what the Saints would say. Max defends her reaction and after some time, she calms down enough to keep going.

On our way out, Gideon asks the orc if he has anything we might use to conceal ourselves. He gives us some of his sister's old clothes.

![[session7_map3.webp]]

With the crowd outside somewhat dispersed, we make our way back to the main elevator. Kat pulls out her tinkers tools and is able to fix it without much fuss.

This primary elevator has stops at different "Tiers" of the tower. Each tier contains multiple floors with various access points. With the elevator now working, Kat can see that 3 of the tiers as well as the penthouse are restricted to keycard access. The elevator will stop on those floors, but the doors won't open without the right access. However, this should be enough to complete [[Elevator Eulogy]]. Also worth noting this elevator is huge (per Oog, the size of a cul-de-sac though honestly it seems even bigger than that).

Kat tries to break into one of the restricted floors with her Tinkers Tools - nope.

We head all the way up to the Penthouse. On the way the elevator stops and a TV mounted near us starts to spark. It comes on and we see the [[Vinny Brassjaw|Suit Goblin]] from before, who asks what's up and why we're fucking around in his elevator.

Silas says we're looking for a way to lie low for a minute. Gideon keeps trying to imply that we're doing drugs. Wink wink.

Suit Goblin basically says to do whatever we want, but he's watching us. This doesn't seem ominous so much as 

Silas brings up our "[[Stalker|stalker]]" and asks him to keep an eye out.

Suit Goblin says we should have this meeting face to face but it'll only happen if we're competent enough to find him. Silas checks behind the TV. Nothing there.

Kat and Silas teach Gideon not to actually say the words "wink wink" when he's trying to surreptitiously imply things.

We settle down for the night in the elevator. On watch, Kat and Dorian talk about his parents and murder. Dorian's philosophy on other people dying boils down to: "I live another day."

They see a small child a ways away wearing a goblin mask, who makes eye contact and waves at them. Kat and Dorian decide to follow. 

The kid leads them to an RV, knocks on the door, and then crawls underneath. Kat looks under the RV and sees no sign of the kid, but the goblin mask is sticking out of the dirt. She takes it. 

After making sure Dorian's cool with it, she knocks on the door of the RV the same way the goblin did. It opens immediately on its own, and is clearly bigger on the inside.  

We see five chairs, three of which are occupied by the sleeping party we left at the car.

Dorian tells Kat to stay there and runs back to check the car. It's empty.

When he comes back, we decide to sit in the remaining two chairs.

The [[Vinny Brassjaw|Suited Goblin]] comes in. Kat asks if he's "Brassjaw" and he says yes.

He says we can call him Vinny. He basically wants to know what we're up to in his tower.

He's received some videos that corroborate our stalker story. We explain the Box Boys thing.

Silas tries to suss out what Brassjaw is after and if he's with the [[Bellanova Family|Bellanovas]]. 

Brassjaw clearly did some research on us and knows quite a bit, though it's unclear if he's aware of [[Hood Station/Hood Station|Hood Station]].

Silas asks directly about Brassjaw working with the [[Ghosts of the Flooded Zone|Ghosts]]. He refers to Commander Torreo as a "he" and when Brassjaw corrects it to "she," Silas puts together the pieces that the Ghosts Commander Torreo is his childhood friend [[Kali Torreo]].

Silas asks Brassjaw to arrange a meeting but gets the response that we need a closer relationship first.

Silas asks what we'd need to do to strengthen the relationship. Brassjaw says the best start would be to do some work for him. He gets our contact info and says he'll start routing some work our way through the Jobber app. No 

Kat stays back, tells Brassjaw she won't kill anyone for him, and tells him a little more about some of the stuff the party's received videos from the stalker about. He says he's not going to ask her to assassinate anyone or anything, so if she can complete jobs non-lethally it's fine with him.

Also, if we do some work for him we might be able to help with [[Malcolm Oublier|Malcolm]]'s debt situation.
