# Description

A section of floors has gone completely dark. No power draw, no comm signals, no movement. A routine check team was sent and never returned. The elevators refuse to stop on those levels, as if manually overridden from within. 

Gain access to the affected floors via maintenance shafts or external climb routes. Determine what caused the blackout and restore control of the systems. Recover or account for the missing team.

### Completion Requirements

- [x] Find cause of blackout
- [x] Restore power
- [x] Recover or account for missing team
