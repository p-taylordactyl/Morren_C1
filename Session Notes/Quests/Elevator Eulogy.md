# Description

The central lift, the spine of the [[BB Megatower|megatower]], has become erratic. It skips floors, opens to empty shafts, and recently crushed a supply team when it activated without command. Some claim the control system has been tampered with. Others say something is nesting in the shaft itself.

Enter the central elevator shaft, inspect the lift mechanisms, and restore safe operation. Remove any hazards, mechanical or otherwise.

### Completion Requirements

- [ ] Restore safe operation of the elevator
- [ ] Remove any hazards
