# Description

Several children who scavenge near the upper grates have gone missing. Those who returned speak of whispers echoing through the pipes, calling them deeper into the dark. The entrances they used are too narrow for most adults, but something down there is guiding them.

Investigate the upper access ducts and trace the source of the voices. Locate any missing children and return them safely. Identify and neutralize whatever is luring them.

### Completion Requirements

- [x] locate the missing children
- [ ] identify and neutralize whatever is luring them
